# Agent 1 Note

## Angle

Compare Japan’s travel seasons by weather comfort, scenery, and crowd pressure for a first 5-day itinerary.

## Findings

- Spring, especially late March to mid-April, is iconic for cherry blossoms and mild weather, but it is also one of the busiest and most expensive periods.
- Late April to early May overlaps with Golden Week, one of Japan’s peak domestic travel periods; avoid it for a short first trip unless dates are fixed.
- Summer brings festivals and green landscapes, but June is rainy season in much of Japan, July-August are hot and humid, and mid-August Obon increases domestic crowds.
- Autumn, especially October-November, often has the best balance: comfortable weather, fall foliage, food season, and strong sightseeing conditions, though popular foliage spots still get crowded.
- Winter is generally the quietest season outside New Year holidays and ski areas; it can be a good value choice for Tokyo/Kyoto/Osaka if colder weather and shorter daylight are acceptable.

- Check JNTO/Japan Travel pages for 2026 Golden Week, Obon, cherry blossom, and autumn foliage timing once forecasts are available.
