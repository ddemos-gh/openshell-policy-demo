# Agent 2 Note

## Angle

A first 5-day Japan itinerary should prioritize depth over coverage, with Tokyo as the anchor and Kyoto added only if the traveler accepts a faster pace.

## Findings

- For most first-timers, the strongest split is either **5 days in Tokyo with one day trip** or **3 days Tokyo + 2 days Kyoto**; adding more cities usually creates too much transit friction.
- **Tokyo-only** works well for travelers interested in food, neighborhoods, shopping, museums, nightlife, and easy day trips such as Kamakura, Nikko, or Hakone.
- **Tokyo + Kyoto** gives a more classic first-Japan contrast: modern urban Japan in Tokyo, then temples, gardens, old streets, and traditional architecture in Kyoto.
- A 5-day trip should generally avoid adding Osaka, Hiroshima, or multiple “beyond” stops unless there is a very specific interest; these are better for a 7-10 day itinerary.
- If flying into Tokyo and out of Osaka, the Tokyo/Kyoto split becomes much more efficient; if round-tripping through Tokyo, Kyoto costs more time and energy.

## Evidence To Gather Next

- Check current Shinkansen travel time, pricing, and luggage reservation rules between Tokyo and Kyoto.
- Compare flight options: Tokyo round trip versus open-jaw arrival in Tokyo and departure from Osaka Kansai or Itami.

## Open Questions

- Is the traveler more interested in cities, food, and pop culture, or temples, gardens, and traditional streets?
- Are flights already fixed, and if so, are they arriving and departing from Tokyo?
