# Agent 3 Note

## Angle

Focus on the letter **C** as a flexible letter whose sound, shape, and role shift across writing systems and contexts.

## Findings

- **C has multiple common sounds** in English, most notably hard /k/ as in “cat” and soft /s/ as in “city.”
- **Its shape descends from earlier alphabets**, with roots often traced through Latin, Etruscan, Greek gamma, and Phoenician gimel.
- **C overlaps with K and S**, making it one of the more orthographically ambiguous English letters.
- **C is highly productive in abbreviations and symbols**, including Celsius, copyright, carbon, century, and musical note C.
- **In grading and ranking systems**, C often signals average or middle performance rather than excellence or failure.
