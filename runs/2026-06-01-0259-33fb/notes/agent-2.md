# Agent 2 Note

## Angle

The angle is the role of the letter **B** as a linguistic, symbolic, and categorizing marker.

## Findings

- **B** is the second letter of the modern English alphabet and descends from Semitic, Greek, and Latin letter traditions.
- In English, **B** most often represents the voiced bilabial stop sound, as in “bat” or “blue.”
- **B** is widely used as a ranking or grading symbol, usually indicating second tier, above average, or secondary status.
- In music, **B** names a pitch in English-speaking notation systems, though historical naming differs across some European traditions.
- As a label, **B** often signals sequence, type, or category: Plan B, Vitamin B, blood type B, B-side.
