# Agent 1 Note

## Angle

Compare Japan’s seasons by comfort, scenery, weather risk, and crowd levels for a first 5-day visit.

## Findings

- Spring, especially late March to early April, is iconic for cherry blossoms but also one of the busiest and most expensive times to visit; timing varies by city and year.
- Autumn, roughly late October through November, is often the best balance: mild weather, fall colors, lower weather risk, and crowds that are busy but usually less intense than peak cherry blossom season.
- Summer is hot, humid, and can be draining for a short first trip, especially in Tokyo, Kyoto, and Osaka; June also brings rainy season, while late summer into early autumn carries typhoon risk.
- Winter is generally quieter and cheaper outside New Year, with crisp weather in major cities and good visibility, but shorter daylight and less garden/temple greenery may make it feel less “classic” for a first visit.
- Major domestic holiday periods can sharply increase crowds and prices, especially Golden Week in late April to early May, Obon in mid-August, and New Year around late December to early January.
