# Agent 1 Note

## Angle

The flavor case for pineapple on pizza depends on whether sweet acidity can balance salty, fatty, savory toppings without overwhelming the base.

## Findings

- Pineapple and ham follow a classic sweet-and-salty pattern: pineapple brings sugar and bright acidity, while ham adds salt, smoke, and umami.
- The pairing works best when pineapple is used as an accent, not the dominant topping; too much juice or sweetness can flatten the cheese and tomato flavors.
- Pizza already supports contrast: tomato sauce is sweet-acidic, mozzarella is fatty and mild, and browned crust adds bitterness and roast notes.
- Heat changes pineapple’s role: baking concentrates sweetness, softens sharp acidity, and can create light caramelized notes that fit with cured meat.
- The combination is more coherent with savory anchors like ham, bacon, jalapeno, red onion, or chili flakes than with pineapple alone.
