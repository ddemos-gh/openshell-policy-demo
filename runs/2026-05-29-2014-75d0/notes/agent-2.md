# Agent 2 Note

## Angle

Internet recipe consensus: identify which soups repeatedly surface as “best” across recipe sites, ratings lists, and home-cook discussions.

## Findings

- Chicken noodle soup remains the safest consensus pick because it combines comfort, broad familiarity, and many highly rated quick/easy versions.
- French onion soup appears often in “most popular” curated recipe lists, including RICARDO’s winter soup rankings, suggesting strong appeal when richness and depth matter.
- Creamy tortellini soup has strong internet momentum, especially in slow-cooker and NYT-adjacent recipe discussions, because it feels indulgent but easy.
- Lentil or red lentil soup is a practical “best everyday soup” candidate: inexpensive, plant-forward, fast, and adaptable with cumin, tomato paste, chile, lemon, or herbs.
- Recipe-site popularity does not equal culinary “best”; it favors approachable ingredients, low effort, and comfort-food familiarity.
