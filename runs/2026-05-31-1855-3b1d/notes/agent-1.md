# Agent 1 Note

## Angle

Location-focused overview of where true buffalo naturally live and which regions define their native habitat.

## Findings

- African buffalo are native to sub-Saharan Africa, especially savannas, woodlands, floodplains, and forest edges where permanent water is available.
- Wild water buffalo are native to South and Southeast Asia, with remnant populations mainly associated with India, Nepal, Bhutan, Thailand, Cambodia, and nearby wetland regions.
- Buffalo habitats are strongly tied to water access; both African buffalo and wild water buffalo tend to occupy areas near rivers, marshes, swamps, floodplains, or seasonal water sources.
- African buffalo have a broader natural range across varied African ecosystems, while wild water buffalo now have a much more fragmented and reduced range.
- The term “buffalo” is sometimes used for American bison, but bison are not true buffalo and are naturally associated with North American grasslands and prairies.
